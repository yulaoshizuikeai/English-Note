# 就近原则与就远原则

外研版选择性必修第三册 Unit 4 重点语法。

---

## 一、就远原则

当主语后跟有以下伴随介词短语时，谓语动词由**前面的主语**决定：
- `with, together with, along with`
- `as well as`
- `rather than`
- `accompanied by, including, except`

> *The professor, as well as his assistants, **was** working on the project.*

---

## 二、就近原则

当由以下连词连接两个并列主语时，谓语动词由**靠近它的主语**决定：
- `either... or...`
- `neither... nor...`
- `not only... but also...`
- `there be` 句型

> *Neither the students nor the teacher **was** late for class.*
