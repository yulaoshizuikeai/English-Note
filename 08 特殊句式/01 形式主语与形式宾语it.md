# 形式主语与形式宾语 it

外研版选择性必修第二册 Unit 4~5 重点语法。

---

## 一、形式主语 it

用 it 代替过长的不定式短语、动名词短语或名词从句：
- `It is + adj. + for sb to do sth.`（修饰事物特征：easy, difficult, important）
- `It is + adj. + of sb to do sth.`（修饰人的品格：kind, wise, polite）
- `It is + reported / believed / thought + that...`

---

## 二、形式宾语 it

公式：**`主语 + find / make / consider / think / feel + it + 宾补 + 真正宾语 (to do / that从句)`**
- *The Internet makes it convenient to acquire knowledge.*
- *We find it necessary that everyone should know the safety rules.*
