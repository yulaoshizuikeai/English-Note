# 考点：虚拟语气在高考与应用文中的应用

在高考英语应用文（如建议信、倡议书）中，合理使用虚拟语气能够增强语气的委婉度与书面感：
- *It is high time that we took effective measures to protect wildlife.*
- *I would appreciate it if you could consider my proposal.*
- *If I were in your shoes, I would communicate with them frankly.*
