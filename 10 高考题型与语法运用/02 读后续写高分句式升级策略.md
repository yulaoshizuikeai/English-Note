# 读后续写高分句式升级策略

在读后续写中，恰当运用高级句式能有效提升语言维度：

## 一、分词伴随与动作链升级

- *普通句*：He walked into the room. He took off his coat and sat down.
- *升级句*：**Walking** into the room, he took off his coat and sat down with a relieved sigh.

## 二、非限制性定语从句渲染环境与心理

- *升级句*：The warm sunlight filtered through the leaves, **which** filled him with a renewed sense of hope.

## 三、独立主格与 with 复合结构

- *升级句*：**With his heart pounding wildly in his chest**, he reached out to open the door.
