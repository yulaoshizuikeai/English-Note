# 应用文写作核心句型模板

## 一、建议信与倡议书

- *It is strongly recommended that we take active steps to...*
- *I would be more than delighted if my suggestions could be of some help to you.*
- *If I were in your position, I would focus on...*

## 二、申请信与自我介绍

- *What makes me suitable for the position is my proficiency in English and strong communication skills.*
- *I would appreciate it if you could grant me the opportunity of an interview.*
